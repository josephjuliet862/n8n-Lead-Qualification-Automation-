# AI Lead Qualification Automation

##  Project Overview

This workflow automates the process of capturing, qualifying, storing, and routing new business leads.

Instead of manually reviewing every lead, the system uses automation and AI to evaluate incoming leads and take the appropriate action.

## 🔧 Tools Used

* n8n
* OpenAI
* Google Sheets
* Gmail

##  How It Works

1. A new lead enters the system.
2. The lead information is captured automatically.
3. AI analyzes and qualifies the lead.
4. Lead information is stored in Google Sheets.
5. Qualified leads are routed appropriately.
6. An automated email notification is sent.
7. The team can follow up without manually processing every new lead.

##  Business Benefits

* Reduces repetitive manual work
* Speeds up lead qualification
* Keeps lead information organized
* Improves response time
* Creates a consistent lead management process
* Helps teams focus on high-value opportunities

##  Automation Logic

**New Lead → Capture → AI Qualification → Store Data → Route Lead → Notify Team → Follow Up**

##  Outcome

The workflow creates a streamlined lead management process that allows businesses to respond to potential customers faster while reducing administrative work.
