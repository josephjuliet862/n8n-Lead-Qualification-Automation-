# GoHighLevel Client Acquisition System

##  Project Overview

A complete client acquisition system built in GoHighLevel to help businesses capture, organize, nurture, and manage leads from one centralized platform.

The system combines a lead-generation funnel, CRM pipeline, forms, appointment booking, and automated follow-up to create a streamlined client journey.

##  Tools Used

- GoHighLevel
- CRM
- Funnel Builder
- Forms
- Calendar
- Workflow Automation
- Email Automation

##  How It Works

1. A potential client visits the landing page.
2. The prospect submits the lead capture form.
3. The contact is automatically added to the CRM.
4. The lead enters the appropriate sales pipeline.
5. The workflow processes the new lead.
6. An automated welcome email is sent.
7. The prospect can book an appointment through the connected calendar.
8. The lead can then be tracked through the sales pipeline.

##  Sales Pipeline

The CRM pipeline was structured to track leads through different stages of the customer journey, making it easier to monitor opportunities and follow up at the right time.

##  Key Features

- Lead capture funnel
- CRM contact management
- Custom contact fields
- Sales pipeline
- Automated lead follow-up
- Appointment booking
- Calendar integration
- Automated welcome email
- Workflow-based lead management

##  Results & Impact

- Centralized lead information in one CRM
- Reduced repetitive manual lead-management tasks
- Created a structured lead follow-up process
- Made appointment booking easier for prospects
- Improved visibility into the sales pipeline
- Created a scalable foundation for automated client acquisition
